Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Ngneerx Technology LLC ("Ngneerx," "we," "us," or "our") operates mobile and web applications including Jam Clash. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our apps and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Device information — device type, operating system, and app version for diagnostics and compatibility.
  • Purchase information — in-app purchase history processed through Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
  • Usage data — anonymous information about how you interact with the app, such as features used and game sessions played, to help us improve the experience.
  • Voice data — if you use Voice Answers, audio is processed on-device or by your platform's speech recognition service (Apple or Google). We do not record or store your voice.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our apps
  • To process in-app purchases and restore them across devices
  • To diagnose technical issues and fix bugs
  • To understand how features are used so we can build better products

3. Third-Party Services

Our apps use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases and subscriptions. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Firebase — used for app infrastructure. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — app distribution and payment processing.
  • iTunes Search API — used to retrieve song previews. No personal data is sent to Apple.

4. Data Storage

Purchase records and app preferences are stored locally on your device using secure storage. We do not operate our own servers that store personally identifiable information.

5. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To make a request, contact us at the email below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
